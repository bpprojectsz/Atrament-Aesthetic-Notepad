Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Atrament is built to work entirely on your device. Your notes, notebooks, and settings are stored locally using SQLite. Atrament itself never sends them anywhere unless you explicitly export or share them yourself. There is no account, no login, and no Atrament server that stores or has access to your note content. Your phone’s own backup service may keep a copy — see “Backups, note lock and diagnostics” below.

Advertising (AdMob)

Free-tier users see a small banner ad on the notebook screen and in Settings, and an occasional full-screen ad after some actions, such as saving, exporting or deleting. A full-screen ad is shown at most once every 9 minutes. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device’s advertising identifier to serve relevant ads.

Your consent choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and in some US states), Google’s consent form is shown so you can choose how your data is used for ads. Where Google says a privacy-choices entry is required, Settings shows a Privacy choices row that reopens the form so you can review or change your answer at any time. If you decline, you can still use all of Atrament. You may still see ads, but Google applies its own limits to what is served (for example limited or non-personalized ads) according to your choice.

If the form cannot be shown. If the consent form cannot be displayed (for example, there is no network), Atrament still asks Google’s consent library for the status it has on record, and Google applies the choice you saved last. If that status cannot be read at all, Atrament requests ads anyway, Google applies its own rules for the consent status it has on record, and Atrament asks again once the next time you return to the app.

iOS. After you first save a note, iOS may ask whether Atrament may track you across other companies’ apps and websites (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt). You can decline without losing any app functionality; if you decline, Google’s ads library cannot use the iOS advertising identifier. Android. Atrament declares the Android advertising ID permission for ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Settings.

Removing ads. The ad-removal purchase stops banner and full-screen ads. Atrament may still start Google’s ads library and show the consent message on a device that has purchased ad removal.

In-App Purchases

The ad-removal one-time purchase is processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit or Google Play Billing. Atrament never collects or stores your payment card details — that information is handled exclusively by Apple or Google.

Reminders, fonts and network use

Daily verse reminder. If you turn it on, Atrament asks your device for permission to show notifications and schedules one notification a day, at the time you choose, on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. The verses are included in the app, and no text is downloaded for them.

Fonts. All fonts Atrament uses are included in the app. Nothing is downloaded for them.

Network use. Atrament’s own code does not contact any server. The only network traffic comes from Google’s ads library and consent form, and from the Apple or Google purchase service when you buy or restore ad removal. Links you tap (this policy, terms, support, rating Atrament, sharing the app) open in your browser or the store.

Backups, note lock and diagnostics

Backups. Atrament does not run its own cloud backup. However, your operating system may include the app’s data in its automatic backups (Google’s device backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iOS). Those backups are controlled by your device and account settings and governed by Google’s or Apple’s terms; Atrament cannot see or access them.

Note lock. The optional note lock uses Face ID, fingerprint, or your device passcode. While it is on, Atrament hides note previews and asks you to unlock before opening, sharing, or exporting a note. It is a screen lock, not encryption: the notes stored on your device are not separately encrypted by Atrament.

Diagnostics. Atrament keeps a small technical error log on your device. It is never sent automatically. If you choose “Share debug log” in Settings, you decide where it goes; it may contain technical details about an error.

Data retention and deletion

Atrament keeps no copy of your notes on any server. Uninstalling Atrament deletes the notes stored on your device. If your device backs up app data (see above), a copy may remain in that backup under your Google or Apple account, which you can manage in your device or account settings. If you have questions about advertising-related data held by Google AdMob, see Google's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Atrament is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or similar regulations to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since Atrament stores your note content on your own device and has no server copy, you already have direct control over it. Any operating system backup of it is managed in your device or account settings. For questions about advertising identifiers or purchase records held by Google or Apple, please contact those providers directly.
