Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Atrament is built to work entirely on your device. Your notes, notebooks, and settings are stored locally using SQLite and never leave your phone unless you explicitly export or share them yourself. There is no account, no login, and no Atrament server that stores or has access to your note content.

What data is collected

Category What's collected Why Shared with
Note content Titles, text, handwriting strokes, paper/font choices Core app functionality — stored locally only Nobody. Never transmitted anywhere.
Purchases Ad-removal subscription status To determine whether to show ads and unlock ad-free use Apple App Store / Google Play Billing (processes the transaction; Atrament never sees your payment details)

Biometric data None collected or stored by Atrament Face ID / fingerprint authentication happens entirely within the operating system — Atrament only receives a yes/no result Nobody

Advertising (AdMob)

Free-tier users see a single small banner ad on the home screen, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device's advertising identifier to serve relevant ads. On iOS, this only happens if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can decline without losing any app functionality. Subscribing to remove ads stops all ad requests entirely.

In-App Purchases

The ad-removal subscription is processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit or Google Play Billing. Atrament never collects or stores your payment card details — that information is handled exclusively by Apple or Google.

Data retention and deletion

Since all note content lives only on your device, uninstalling Atrament permanently deletes it — there is no cloud copy to separately request deletion of. If you have questions about advertising-related data held by Google AdMob, see Google's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Atrament is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or similar regulations to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since Atrament stores your note content only on your own device, you already have full, direct control over it at all times. For questions about advertising identifiers or purchase records held by Google or Apple, please contact those providers directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
