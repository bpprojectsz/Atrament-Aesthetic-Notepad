Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Atrament works entirely offline with no sign-up, ever.

Will my notes sync across devices?

Not currently. Atrament is offline-first by design — everything is stored locally on your device using SQLite.

What does removing ads actually unlock?

Nothing extra. Every feature — handwriting, all paper textures, all fonts, exports, biometric lock, notifications, unlimited notes — is already free. Removing ads just removes the single banner on the home screen.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases." This uses your Apple ID or Google account purchase history — no login to Atrament itself is required.

How do I lock a note with Face ID or fingerprint?

Go to Settings → Privacy and enable "Lock notes with Face ID / fingerprint." This requires your device to already have biometric authentication set up in its own system settings.

Can I turn off the daily verse?

Yes. Go to Settings → Daily Verse Display and choose "Off." You can also disable the daily reminder notification separately under Notifications.

Troubleshooting

The app won't open notifications on time

Check that notifications are allowed for Atrament in your device's system settings, and that battery optimization isn't preventing scheduled notifications from firing on Android.

Exported PDF or text file didn't appear

Exports hand off to your device's native share sheet. Look for the app you selected in that sheet (Files, Mail, etc.) — Atrament doesn't save exports to a fixed location on its own.

A paper texture or font looks wrong

Try switching to a different paper style and back in the note editor. If the issue persists, please contact support above with your device model and OS version.
