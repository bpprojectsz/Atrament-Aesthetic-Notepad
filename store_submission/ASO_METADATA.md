## Full Description (both stores, 4000 char max)

Atrament is a premium, distraction-free notepad built for people who miss
the feel of real paper.

Choose from seven paper textures — lined, dot grid, engineering grid,
blank, cream, aged parchment, and smooth vellum — and write with either
rich text or genuine freehand handwriting using a fountain pen, gel pen,
pencil, or highlighter.

A quiet daily verse can appear as a faint watermark, header, or footer —
entirely optional, and off by default if it's not for you.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud sync, no data
collection beyond what's needed to show a single small ad to free users.
Lock any note with Face ID or fingerprint. Export to PDF or plain text
and share however you like.

Every feature is free, permanently — handwriting, every paper texture,
every font, unlimited notes, exports, biometric lock, and notifications.
Removing the one home-screen banner ad costs $0.99/month or $9.99/year
and doesn't unlock anything else, because there's nothing left to unlock.

Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi,
Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and Hebrew.
